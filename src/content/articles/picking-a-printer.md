Most "best printer" lists are written by people who haven't lived with the machines for more than a few weeks. The questions that actually matter only show up after a few hundred hours of printing.

## Build volume is the last thing to optimise for

Almost every beginner overestimates how much volume they need. A 220x220 bed prints 90% of what gets posted online.

## Reliability beats speed

A fast printer that needs babysitting will outproduce a slow one only in marketing copy. In practice, a boring machine that prints 24/7 wins.
