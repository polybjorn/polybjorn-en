Slicers can compensate for a lot, but the cheapest way to improve a print is to design the part with FDM in mind from the start.

## Orient the part before you model the part

Before adding a single feature, decide which face is down. Every chamfer, overhang, and bolt hole afterwards is informed by that choice.

## 45 degrees is a soft rule, not a hard one

Modern slicers handle 50 to 55 degree overhangs cleanly with the right cooling. Use that headroom to avoid supports entirely on small parts.
